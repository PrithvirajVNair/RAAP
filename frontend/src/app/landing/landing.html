What is This Platform?

Our Risk Assessment Platform helps companies identify, manage, and mitigate risks effectively.

Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)

Admins and Managers oversee risk creation and assignment, Workers focus on mitigation tasks, and Auditors have read-only access for compliance and reporting.

Smart Risk Scoring & Tracking

Risks are automatically classified as Low, Medium, or High, with real-time status tracking from Open to Mitigated, along with clear ownership and defined due dates.

Complete Audit & Risk History

Every risk update is recorded with detailed history, secure audit logs track who did what and when, and compliance-ready reports support auditing requirements.

Whether you are managing operational, security, or business risks - this platform keeps everything organized, transparent, and controlled.
